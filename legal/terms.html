What the app is not

Microbarium is a game. The Gunk Meter is a playful estimate based on how much you have handled your phone — it is not a measurement of your screen, and nothing in the app is medical, hygienic or health advice. The care tips are ordinary device-care suggestions; follow your manufacturer's guidance for cleaning your own hardware.
