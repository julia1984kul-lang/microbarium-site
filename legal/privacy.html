Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Microbarium is a screen-cleaning game. We built it to be fun, not to learn things about you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no name or email is ever required to use the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app lives on your iPhone and is never uploaded:

  • your cleaning history, streaks and daily goals
  • the microbes you have discovered, your favourites, and your collection progress
  • your in-game Gunk level and the step count it is based on
  • your settings, sounds, and chosen brush trail

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What we do collect

Anonymous usage statistics. We record which screens are opened and which actions are taken — for example, that a cleaning session was finished, or that a paywall was shown. This is tied to a random identifier generated by the app, not to you. It tells us which parts of the game work and which do not.

A random device identifier. Created by the app itself. It lets us count returning players and keep your subscription attached to your device. It is not your Apple ID, not your phone number, and it is not the advertising identifier — we never request permission to track you, because we do not.

Subscription status. Whether you are Free or Pro, and whether a trial is active. Purchases themselves are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Crash and performance reports. If the app crashes, we receive an anonymous technical report so we can fix it.

What we never collect

Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location of any kind, health data, or the contents of your screen. The app never asks iOS for your location, and we have switched off the network-address lookup our analytics providers would otherwise use to guess your country or city — so we do not receive even that. The app has no camera or microphone access. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Motion data

With your permission, the app reads your iPhone's step count and daily activity summary. It uses this to guess how busy your day has been, and turns that into the game's Gunk level — the number that decides when the app suggests a clean.

To be clear about what this is not: the app does not and cannot inspect your screen, and nothing here is a real measurement of dirt. It is a playful estimate based on how much you moved around, nothing more.

Step data is processed on your device only and is never transmitted. You can refuse or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app keeps working, it just falls back to a simple time-based guess.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app can send optional reminders. You can turn them off in iOS Settings or inside the app.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Remote Config) — crash reports and feature configuration. Firebase Analytics is bundled but its data collection is switched off.
  • Amplitude — usage statistics
  • RevenueCat — subscription status
  • Apple — payments, and the App Store's own anonymous statistics

These providers act on our behalf under their own privacy terms. None of them receives information that identifies you personally.

Children

Microbarium is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

How long we keep it

Anonymous statistics are retained for up to 24 months and then deleted. On-device data lives as long as the app is installed.

Your rights

Because we hold no information that identifies you, we usually cannot find "your" records on request. You can still delete everything at any time by deleting the app. If you are in the EU, UK or California and want to ask about your data, write to us and we will help as far as the anonymous nature of the data allows.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and note it in the app's release notes.
