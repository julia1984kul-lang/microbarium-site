Microbarium

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Microbarium is a screen-cleaning game. We built it to be fun, not to learn things about you. There are no accounts and no sign-up, and the app never asks for your name or email.

What stays on your device

Most of what you do in the app lives only on your iPhone and is never sent to us:

  • your cleaning history, streaks and daily goals
  • the microbes you have discovered, your favourites, and your collection progress
  • your in-game Gunk level and the step count it is based on
  • your settings, sounds, and chosen brush trail

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Two small notes are kept in your iPhone's secure storage (the Keychain) and survive a reinstall: your Player ID, and a note that you have already received the free Pro cleans.

Your Player ID

When you first open the app, it creates a random Player ID — ten letters and numbers, for example K7F3Q-92XM4. It is not your Apple ID, your phone number or the advertising identifier, and on its own it says nothing about who you are. We use it to:

  • keep usage statistics and your subscription status together, so we can tell a returning player from a new one;
  • find your records when you write to support — the Contact Support email in Settings adds your Player ID for you;
  • send you a gift, such as a microbe or Pro time, when we choose to.

What we collect

Usage statistics. We record which screens are opened and which actions are taken — for example, that a cleaning session was finished, or that a paywall was shown. These are filed under your Player ID, together with basic technical details: iPhone model, iOS version, app version and language. They tell us which parts of the game work and which do not.

Subscription status. Whether you are Free or Pro, whether a trial is active, and the purchase records Apple shares with our subscription provider: which plan, when it started and renews, and your App Store country. Purchases themselves are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Feedback you send us. The app sometimes asks a short question after a clean, and Settings has a "Share an Idea" box you can open whenever you like. Both are optional — you can close them and nothing is sent. If you do answer, we store what you tapped and anything you wrote, along with your app version, iOS version, whether you are Free or Pro, how many cleans and clean days you have, how long ago you installed the app, and — if the question is about why you cancelled Pro — whether it was a trial or a paid month. Your Player ID is not attached, so we cannot trace an answer back to you, and we cannot reply to it. We read these to decide what to build next.

Emails you send us. If you write to support@microbarium.com, we receive your email address, your name if your mail app includes it, what you wrote, and the details the app adds to the draft: your Player ID, app version and iOS version. We use these only to answer you and to fix your problem.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, we receive a technical report — what went wrong in the code, your iPhone model and iOS version — so we can fix it. It does not carry your Player ID.

Location. The app never asks for your location. Like any internet service, our providers see your network address when the app connects to them; we have switched off the analytics features that store it or work out a city or region from it.

What we never collect

The app never asks for your contacts, photos, location or health data, and it never sees the contents of your screen. It has no camera or microphone access. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps and websites, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Motion data

With your permission, the app reads your iPhone's step count and daily activity summary. It uses this to guess how busy your day has been, and turns that into the game's Gunk level — the number that decides when the app suggests a clean.

To be clear about what this is not: the app does not and cannot inspect your screen, and nothing here is a real measurement of dirt. It is a playful estimate based on how much you moved around, nothing more.

Step data is processed on your iPhone only and never leaves it. You can refuse or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app keeps working, it just falls back to a simple time-based guess.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app can send optional reminders. They are created on your iPhone, and nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in iOS Settings or inside the app.

Why we use your data

  • Usage statistics, crash reports and feature switches — we have a legitimate interest in keeping the game working and making it better. You can object at any time (see "Your rights").
  • Subscription status — we need it to give you the Pro features you paid for.
  • Feedback and emails — you choose to send them, and we use them only for the reason you sent them.
  • Step count — used on your iPhone only, with your permission.

Services we use

  • Amplitude — usage statistics (United States)
  • RevenueCat — subscription status (United States)
  • Google Firebase — crash reports (Crashlytics); feature switches and gifts (Remote Config, which receives your Player ID); and the feedback you choose to send (Firestore, stored in the EU). Firebase Analytics comes with Firebase but is switched off.
  • Google (Gmail) and Namecheap — our support inbox, and the forwarding of mail sent to support@microbarium.com
  • Apple — payments, and the App Store's own statistics

These providers process data to run their service for us, under their own privacy terms. When data leaves the EU or the UK, it is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Children

Microbarium is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent and think your child has sent us an email or feedback with personal details, write to us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

  • Usage statistics and subscription records — as long as we need them to run the game and your subscription. We delete yours when you ask.
  • Crash reports — deleted automatically after about 90 days.
  • Feedback — kept while the app is being worked on, because it is what we plan from. It carries no Player ID, so nothing in it points to you.
  • Emails — as long as we need them to help you. We delete them when you ask.
  • On-device data — as long as the app is installed.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it for statistics. The easiest way: in the app, open Settings → Contact Support and tell us what you want. The email already contains your Player ID, which is how we find your records. We answer within one month.

Feedback answers carry no Player ID, so we cannot find or delete a single one. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone, but not the records our providers hold under your Player ID — for those, write to us.

If you are in the EU or the UK and are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law: we do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted ads.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update the date at the top and mention it in the app's release notes.

Who is responsible for your data

Microbarium is made by Yuliia Danylova, an independent developer. "We" and "us" in this policy mean her. She is responsible for the data described here (the "controller" under EU and UK data protection law).
