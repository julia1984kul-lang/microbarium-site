Microbarium

Wipe your screen. Catch what lives on it.

Microbarium turns a boring chore into a hunt. Scrub your iPhone screen and microbes appear under your finger — dodging, hiding, splitting in two. Catch them and they join your collection.

Keep a daily streak and new species unlock over the weeks. It is a game, not a lab: nothing here measures real dirt.
